## 🌟 Project Overview

A world-class, premium website for **Mihen Halani & Associates (MHA)**, a leading practicing company secretaries firm established in 2013.

**Mission Statement:**  
MHA partners with boards and promoters to simplify governance, strengthen compliance, and provide clarity in decision-making across every stage of business growth — from formation to public markets.

**Trusted By:**  
Startups, listed companies, investors, and global businesses operating in India.

**Design Inspiration:** EY India, Deloitte, Bhuta Shah & Associates

---

## 🎯 MHA's Three Pillars

### 1. **Governance**
We work closely with boards to establish robust governance frameworks that support transparent decision‑making, accountability, and long‑term value creation.

### 2. **Compliance**
From routine statutory compliances to complex regulatory frameworks, we ensure seamless compliance under the Companies Act, SEBI regulations, and FEMA — reducing risk and building institutional credibility.

### 3. **Strategic Clarity**
By aligning compliance with business strategy, we help organisations prepare for fundraising, restructuring, and capital market transactions with confidence.
