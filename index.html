About MHA

Clarity in Regulation. Confidence in Decisions

Mihen Halani & Associates (MHA) partners with boards and promoters to build strong, compliant, and future‑ready businesses. Founded in 2013, MHA helps organisations navigate complex regulatory environments with confidence — enabling decisive leadership, transparent governance, and sustainable growth.

We work with startups, growth‑stage companies, listed entities, and international businesses operating in India. Our role goes beyond routine compliance. We simplify governance and align regulatory frameworks with business strategy so that leadership teams can focus on scale and value creation.

What We Stand For

At MHA, values shape vision. We believe robust governance is not a checkbox exercise — it is a strategic asset. Our approach combines compliance discipline with commercial understanding, helping boards take informed decisions even in high‑stakes situations.

Over the past decade, we have advised 1,000+ clients in India and globally, guiding them through critical milestones under Company Law, FEMA, and SEBI regulations.

What We Do

We support businesses across the entire lifecycle — from formation to public markets — through a holistic evaluation of compliance, finance, and strategy.

Our core services include:

  • Corporate governance advisory and board support
  • Business structuring and company formations
  • Fundraising advisory (Preferential Issues, Rights Issues)
  • IPO advisory (Mainboard and SME)
  • Mergers, acquisitions, and corporate reorganisations
  • FEMA and cross‑border transaction advisory
  • Ongoing regulatory compliances under the Companies Act, SEBI laws, and FEMA

Regular and ongoing compliances form the backbone of strong governance. We assist companies with end‑to‑end compliance management under:

  • Companies Act, 2013 – secretarial compliances, board and shareholder processes, ROC filings, and governance reporting
  • SEBI regulations – LODR compliances, insider trading regulations, related‑party transaction frameworks, and listed entity governance
  • FEMA regulations – FDI/FPI compliances, FC‑GPR, FC‑TRS, ODI, ECB, and cross‑border structuring

We also work closely with foreign clients, assisting them in establishing compliant Indian operations and maintaining governance excellence as they scale.

Why MHA

What differentiates MHA is our ability to bridge regulatory clarity with business insight. Our curiosity to understand diverse industries has shaped a strong practice in IPO and primary market transactions, where governance precision must align seamlessly with commercial objectives.

We don't offer generic solutions. Every engagement is structured around the client's business model, growth plans, and regulatory exposure — ensuring clarity, credibility, and long‑term resilience.

Specialized Transaction Experience

MHA has successfully executed a wide range of complex capital market and corporate transactions for listed entities and companies preparing for public markets:

Preferential Issues

Share issuances for cash or consideration other than cash, structured specifically for corporate acquisitions and mergers.

Rights Issues

End-to-end guidance for listed entities issuing rights shares under diverse regulatory capital frameworks.

Investor Representation

Representing companies before institutional investors, deal structuring, and compiling premium corporate pitch decks.

Direct Listings

Smooth migration advisory services from SME listing platforms up to mainboard stock exchanges.

Cross-Exchange Listings

Assisting entities listed on a primary stock exchange to obtain compliant listings on other stock exchanges.

IPO Readiness & Planning

Comprehensive preparation including DRHP drafting support, regulatory board coordination, and capital readiness reviews.

Our hands-on experience with these complex transactions enables us to anticipate regulatory challenges, structure compliant pathways, and provide boards with actionable guidance at every stage of the capital market journey.

Our Services

Governance, Compliance & Strategic Advisory Across the Business Lifecycle

At Mihen Halani & Associates (MHA), we provide end‑to‑end governance, compliance, and transaction advisory services to businesses operating across diverse stages and regulatory environments. Our work is anchored in regulatory clarity, board‑level advisory, and practical business understanding — enabling organisations to grow with confidence.

We serve startups, growth‑stage companies, listed entities, investors, and international businesses operating in India.

Governance & Ongoing Compliances

Long-term governance partnership with boards, covering end-to-end Companies Act compliances, board processes, and regulatory reporting.

SEBI & Listed Entity Advisory

Comprehensive SEBI LODR compliances, insider trading frameworks, related party transactions, and liaison with exchanges and regulators.

FEMA & Cross-Border Advisory

FDI, FPI, ODI, ECB compliances, cross-border structuring, FEMA audits, and advisory on overseas investments.

Business Setup & Structuring

Incorporation of companies and LLPs, business restructuring, branch/liaison office registration, and NCLT representations.

Startups & MSME Advisory

Startup India registrations, early-stage investment support, SHA/SSA/SPA drafting, ESOP structuring, and contract advisory.

Fundraising & Capital Structuring

Preferential issues, rights issues, instrument structuring (CCDs, CCPS), share warrants, and exit planning.

IPO & SME IPO Advisory

End-to-end advisory for Mainboard and SME IPOs, from readiness to post-listing compliance and regulatory coordination.

Mergers, Acquisitions & Restructuring

Complex corporate restructuring, mergers, demergers, share acquisitions, buy-backs, and group-level reorganizations.

Due Diligence & Compliance Reviews

Transaction-specific legal and secretarial due diligence for VC/PE investments, M&A, IPOs, and ECB transactions.

Secretarial Audit

Professional secretarial audits for listed and unlisted companies under the Companies Act, 2013, with timely reporting.

NCLT & Regulatory Matters

NCLT proceedings including revival of struck-off companies, voluntary/compulsory winding-up, and business closure matters.

Transaction Advisory & CFO Support

Integrated transaction and financial advisory including tax, IBC, transfer pricing, fundraising support, and virtual CFO services.

Other Allied Services

IEC, IP registrations, PAN/TAN/GST, charge registration, bank due diligence, and legal opinion services.
