1. Applicability & Scope

Mihen Halani & Associates (“MHA”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is a peer-reviewed firm of Practicing Company Secretaries and corporate governance advisors operating under the statutory framework of the Institute of Company Secretaries of India (ICSI).

This Privacy Policy applies to all individuals, corporate entities, founders, directors, and representatives who access, browse, or communicate through our website or engage our professional services across Board Advisory, Corporate Secretarial, FEMA Compliance, SEBI & Listed Entity Governance, and Capital Market Readiness.

This Policy should be read alongside our Terms & Conditions. By accessing or using our website, submitting queries, or sharing information with us, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and consented to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Data We Collect

We collect both Personal Information and Non-Personal Technical Information when you interact with our website or engage our professional secretarial and governance consultancy services (“Information”).

  • Identity & Contact Information: Name, professional title, corporate designation, email address, telephone number, organization name, DIN/DPIN numbers, and official communication details.
  • Engagement & Regulatory Data: Information relating to corporate filings, board structures, shareholding patterns, transaction documents, or regulatory queries provided for advisory proposals.
  • Technical Telemetry: IP address, device specifications, browser type, operating system, timestamped access logs, and website interaction analytics.

3. Purpose & Use of Data

The Information we collect is processed strictly for legitimate professional and regulatory compliance purposes, including:

  • Formulating, evaluating, and delivering governance advisory, secretarial, FEMA, and capital market proposals.
  • Communicating regulatory alerts, corporate compliance calendars, legal updates, and advisory insights.
  • Verifying stakeholder identity, maintaining statutory registers, and preventing fraudulent or unauthorized activities.
  • Complying with statutory directives under the Companies Act, 2013, SEBI Regulations, RBI/FEMA guidelines, and orders of judicial or quasi-judicial authorities.

4. Confidentiality & Disclosure

Professional Confidentiality Guarantee: As Practicing Company Secretaries, MHA adheres to strict professional secrecy standards prescribed by the ICSI Code of Conduct. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or corporate data to any commercial third party.

We maintain strict confidentiality over all client information. Disclosures are made solely under the following circumstances:

  • When explicitly instructed or authorized by you in connection with client engagements (e.g., filings with ROC, MCA, SEBI, or RBI).
  • Where required by law, court orders, or statutory regulatory authorities under mandatory legal processes.
  • To trusted professional auditors or technical infrastructure partners bound by equivalent non-disclosure obligations.

5. Data Security Protocols

MHA implements robust technical, organizational, and administrative safeguards to protect your Data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or breach.

Our security practices include SSL encryption, role-based access control, periodic vulnerability assessments, and secure cloud storage. In the event of a suspected data security incident, please notify our compliance team immediately at compliance@mha-cs.com.

6. Data Retention

We retain Personal Information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, or as mandated by applicable statutory laws, Company Secretarial standards, and tax regulations in India.

7. Cross-Border Transfers

Where advisory services involve cross-border entities, India Entry setup, or overseas direct investment (ODI/FDI under FEMA), information may be processed or transferred internationally under adequate security safeguards compliant with applicable Data Protection Laws.

8. Cookie Policy

Our website utilizes cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance user experience, evaluate website performance, and remember navigation preferences. You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA) and applicable global data protection principles, you possess rights to:

  • Request confirmation and summary of Personal Data processed by MHA.
  • Request correction, updating, or completion of inaccurate information.
  • Request erasure of Personal Information, subject to statutory retention obligations.
  • Withdraw consent for optional communications or email alerts at any time.

10. Governing Law & Jurisdiction

This Privacy Policy is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India. Any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Policy shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Courts of Mumbai, Maharashtra, India.
